# SoundGeo 0.4.0-alpha.6

Windows now keeps the workbench's text and controls consistent with its dark
panels. A light Windows system theme previously made requirements and progress
messages difficult to read.

The [public EXE field check](../../validation/released-exe-lax-20261117-evaluation.json)
returned a region center 810.41 m from a published LAX filming-site reference.
Its predeclared 200 m microphone-position allowance gives 1,010.41 m, narrowly
missing that test's 1 km rule. Automatic flight downloads and an identical
offline replay completed. The fixed desktop also completed a cold-cache run with
visible download, search and completion messages; its displayed results matched
the baseline. The [separate public CCTV audio check](../../validation/public-appeal-audio-20261128.json)
found insufficient sound evidence and produced no location result.
